The bounds.json cases are adapted from test/data/pmd_bounds at commit
a8b52e069bfd4a7a57434c91bc0470ac03cfdd55 and licensed under CC BY 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ (text in CC-BY-4.0.txt).
Changes: bundled the 12 cases and added explicit schema/frequency metadata;
electrical coefficients and objectives are unchanged. bounds-reference.json
contains derived results from the pinned BMOPFTools IVR engine.

components.json, controls.json, equipment.json and unbalanced.json are independently authored synthetic cases
under Tellegen's MIT license, with generated references from BMOPFTools.

feeder.json is an independently authored four-bus synthetic integration case
under Tellegen's MIT license, combining the existing ZIP, delta-wye transformer
and four-leg inverter control witnesses. feeder-reference.json contains results
from the same pinned BMOPFTools revision; its provenance records input SHA-256,
Julia/JuMP/Ipopt versions, options, units, tolerances and regeneration command.
