Experimental RMS tools in the browser. The engine's RMS worker gains a case's DYR records as named parameters and their edits, a unit's models (set, remove, a published unit), controller block graphs (template, check) and a live session that advances on demand and takes events at its current time (`BrowserRmsLive`); `BrowserRmsCase.linear` takes frequency responses (`bode`). Dynamics mode gains a live workbench under the map (plots of any bus, unit or line channel; bus and line faults, trips, closes, load steps and reference changes applied as they are picked; the engine's real-time factor), a bus popup on the map, unit chips that open a unit editor with block diagrams, and Bode plots.
