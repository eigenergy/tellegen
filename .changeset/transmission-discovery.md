Transmission diagrams show where problems are at the case overview. Loading gauges carry a
leader to their branch, move around it to find space, and mark at-rating and overload by
outline and fill as well as colour. Area shading shows buses near their voltage limits, and
in OPF economics, where congestion separates prices from the median LMP. Hovering a row in a
result list highlights its equipment.

The views are visible tabs instead of a dropdown, and "Map results" is now "Map display". A
"Single-line diagram" button beside the map's zoom controls opens the diagram. The results
panel collapses to its summary and starts collapsed on phones. Load symbols are quieter,
result values are rounded for reading with exact values on hover, and the branch inspector
states the flow in words.
